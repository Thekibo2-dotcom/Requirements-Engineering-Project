# Week 1 - Initial Discovery
## 1. Facts
1. The college wants a system for booking equipment.
2. Bookings are managed by a mixture of emails, spreadsheets and informal arragements
3. There are problems about equipment being double-booked, whether its available and not being returned.
4. Staff is spending time on checking the booking manually
5. Students are having problems knowing who to contact
## 2. Assumptions
1. I assume the college wants a system that can record past uses.
2. I assume the college would want to know who has it and for how long
3. I assume the staff wants a system that is easier to understand and automatic
4. I assume the college wants a simple system and easy to find system where the students can use it.
## 3. Unknowns
1. I don't know if the college wants to keep up with the same system.
2. I don't know how the current system works internally or how information is distributed or saved
3. I don't know how many students and staff use this system.
4. I don't know how many equipment and the exact amount and kind there is
## 4. Stakeholders
1. Students - They want a system that is easy to work with, efficient and availability
2. Staff - They want a system that doesn't need to put everything manually and easy to book equipment.
3. IT specialist - They care about the equipment and would need to keep the equipment up-to-date
4. Government - They care about giving the equipment or supplying the college with funds for the system/equipment
5. College - They want a system for booking equipment and giving the students/staff an easier time.
- Challenges
1. Students might want more control over the equipment and its use, while the IT specialists might want no modification of the settings
## 5. Goals
1. The system should help the students/staff have an easier time booking and understanding what there is available.
2. The system should help the staff managing the back-end of the system.
3. The system should be easy to find and use.
## 6. Scope
- Likely to be a problem
1. It might take a while for students to transition to a new system.
2. Staff might have a problem managing the system
3. Depending on students and staff count there might not be enough equipment for the traffic
- Cannot Currently assume are required
1. A mobile app
2. A database
## 7. Candidate Requirements
- Functional Requirements
1. The system shall allow the users to book equipment at a specific date and time
2. The system shall record bookings
3. The system shall give staff a easy managing system
4. The system shall eliminate the problems the current system is experiencing
- Non-Functional Requirements
1. The system shall be secure so the system wouldn't allow to hoard equipment or allow people to access data
2. The system should be able to support the whole college traffic
## 8. Requirement Surgery
1. The system should be easy to use.
- Explanation - This requirement is weak because it doesn't explain what it needs to change and who it affects, What is an easy system for assuming the users.
- Rewrite - The system should be accessible for the users and solve the problems they are encountering at the current system
2. The system should be secure
- Explanation - This requirement is also weak because it doesn't explain what type of security it needs or what type of vulnerability there is.
- Rewrite - The system should just allow the authorized student users to book and view times while the staff to do what the students can do but view bookings and past records.
