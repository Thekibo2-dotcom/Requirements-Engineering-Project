# Week 2 - Elicitation
## 1. Stakeholders
| Stakeholder | What might they care about? |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| Student | Finding and booking equipment |
| Lecturer | Getting equipment when needed for teaching |
| Equipment technician | Managing equipment and returns |
| Department administrator | Managing bookings and records |
| Equipment manager | Control, availability and accountability |
## 2. Stakeholder needs and concerns
| Stakeholder | Possible need | Possible concern |
| ------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Student | Know whether equipment is available | Being unable to get equipment when needed |
| Lecturer | Have equipment for teaching | Equipment not being available at the required time |
| Technician | Know what equipment is due out and back | Late or missing equipment |
| Equipment Manager | Maintain accountability for equipment | Equipment being lost or used without authorisation |
## 3. Unknowns
| Unknown | Who could answer? |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| Who is allowed to book specialist equipment? | Equipment Manager |
| What happens when equipment is returned late | Equipment technician |
| What happens when equipment is returned damaged | Equipment Manager |
| What happens when all the equipment is booked and there is still demand | Equipment Manager |
## 4. Information sources
Potential Sources: Student, Lecturer, Equipment Technician, Department Administrator, Equipment Manager, College.
## 5. Elicitation questions
| Stakeholder | Questions |
| ----------- | --------- |
| Student | How do you book equipment currently? |
| Lecturer | Have you encountered a time when you were unable to book equipment for a lesson? and why couldn't you |
| Equipment Manager | After booking equipment how long do you have to wait and is there anyone that doesn't need to wait? |
| Equipment Manager | Follow-up: How many pieces of equipment can you book and is there anyone who can book more or just one? |
| Department Manager | What type of records system do you use? |
| College | How much traffic do you get for the system? |
## 6. Interview notes
- Three Things learnt
1. the system gets over 80 bookings monthly by students and 120 bookings monthly by lecturer
2. many students were unwilling to book equipment due to the current method
3. Students are reserved to one booking a month regardless of if they have returned the equipment

- One thing we still don't know
1. We could not pinpoint whether it was human error or a system fault which allowed double-bookings
## 7. Candidate requirement
The system shall - 
1. Prevent overbookings.
2. Allow accessibility for those with impairment
3. Allow everyone to book equipment without direct department interaction
4. The system shall support up to 300 users monthly and 15 users daily.
